# Vision

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision of QuantKimun.

It describes the future state that the project aspires to achieve and serves as a strategic reference for architectural decisions, product evolution and community growth.

Unlike the Founding Charter, which explains why the project exists, this document describes what QuantKimun aims to become.

---

# Vision Statement

**To become a trusted engineering platform for designing, testing, executing and evolving quantitative trading systems through disciplined software engineering, reusable architecture and knowledge-driven development.**

---

# Long-Term Vision

QuantKimun aspires to become a comprehensive platform where quantitative researchers, software engineers and trading professionals can build robust trading solutions without being constrained by a specific broker, platform or technology.

The project seeks to provide a stable and extensible engineering foundation that enables innovation while preserving architectural consistency and long-term maintainability.

Rather than focusing solely on trading strategies, QuantKimun aims to support the entire lifecycle of quantitative trading systems—from research and simulation to execution, monitoring and continuous improvement.

---

# Strategic Objectives

## Engineering Excellence

Promote software engineering best practices in quantitative trading through clean architecture, modular design, automated testing and comprehensive documentation.

---

## Platform Independence

Ensure that business logic remains independent of any specific trading platform, allowing the same domain model to operate across multiple execution environments.

---

## Knowledge Preservation

Treat documentation, architecture and design decisions as strategic assets that evolve together with the software.

Knowledge should remain accessible, traceable and reusable.

---

## Extensibility

Provide a modular architecture where new adapters, execution engines, analytical tools and research components can be integrated without affecting the core domain.

---

## Community

Encourage collaboration through transparent governance, clear documentation and consistent engineering standards.

Every contribution should strengthen the quality and sustainability of the project.

---

# What QuantKimun Is

QuantKimun is:

- an engineering platform;
- a reusable domain model;
- a modular architecture;
- a foundation for quantitative trading applications;
- a long-term software project.

---

# What QuantKimun Is Not

QuantKimun is not:

- a collection of trading strategies;
- a broker-specific solution;
- a NinjaTrader-only framework;
- a promise of trading profitability;
- a shortcut for algorithmic trading success.

---

# Success Criteria

The vision of QuantKimun will be considered successful if, over time, it becomes:

- a trusted engineering foundation for quantitative trading;
- easy to extend and maintain;
- independent of specific technologies;
- understandable by new contributors;
- recognized for its architectural quality and documentation.

---

# Guiding Values

The evolution of QuantKimun shall always prioritize:

- Knowledge over assumptions.
- Architecture over improvisation.
- Discipline over shortcuts.
- Sustainability over rapid growth.
- Quality over quantity.
- Collaboration over individual ownership.

---

# Future Outlook

The project is expected to evolve through incremental iterations.

Future capabilities may include:

- Multiple execution platform adapters.
- Advanced risk management modules.
- Portfolio management.
- Research environments.
- Backtesting engines.
- Optimization tools.
- Analytical dashboards.
- Cloud-native execution services.

These capabilities represent possible directions rather than fixed commitments.
